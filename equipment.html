Optical Systems

// 6 SCOPE CONFIGURATIONS

  • Red Dot Sight

    Point-blank to 100m utility. The fastest ADS acquisition in the game — essential on SMGs, shotguns, and high-speed AR pushes in close quarters.

  • Holographic Sight

    Wider field-of-view than Red Dot with a broader reticle. Better target tracking during spray control. Preferred by aggressive fraggers in compounds.

  • 2× Scope

    Bridges CQC and medium range. Dominant on ARs at 100–200m. Used heavily on M416 and AKM builds for the best engagement versatility across all map types.

  • PREMIUM

    4× Scope

    The most versatile long-range optic. Perfect for DMRs and snipers at 200–400m engagements. The PUBG/BGMI standard for open-field combat dominance.

  • PREMIUM

    6× Scope

    Long-range supremacy for bolt-action snipers. Used on Kar98k and M24 for 400–600m engagements. Best in class for Miramar and Erangel open fields.

  • PREMIUM

    8× Scope

    Reserved for AWM and sniper setups in final zones. Extreme 600m+ range engagement. Rare drop — a priority loot objective in any match.
